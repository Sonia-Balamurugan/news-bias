# Development Notes

## Initial Idea
A tool powered by LangChain that compares how different outlets frame stories in a domain, identify which perspectives are highlighted, which get missed etc.

## Design Decisions
### Domain
AI

### Bias Criteria
* Are both benefits and risks discussed? Is there an emphasis on one over the other?
* Whose perspective dominates? Stakeholders to consider include academic researchers, corporate researchers, company executives, regulators, policy advisors, general public, professional users, professionals affected etc.
*  Are claims sourced to research / data or to press releases and speculation?
* Is language measured or emotionally loaded?
* Is language used factual and representative of machine processes or drawing unwarranted parallels to human experiences / processes?

## Design Considerations
### Choosing a Domain
Factors considered in choosing a domain
* Personal interest
* News Volume and Source Diversity
* Variance in Perspectives
* Feasibility of bias criteria
* Data Availability
* Interest / Value
* Stability of bias patterns

### Bias Criteria
Several dimensions of bias can be considered...
* Selection Bias: which topics receive disproportionate attention; relative coverage of news aspects
* Framing Bias: how news stories are presented; includes lean (alignment with certain viewpoints) and tone
* Linguistic Bias: choice of words used
* Statement Bias: relative polarity of reported news aspects
* Perspective selection
